ZYAN Ming Regular and SemiBold are derived from Adobe Source Han Serif SC.
Source commit: 7889f11bf31170b5d092a083b357c8c8130f89e0
https://github.com/adobe-fonts/source-han-serif
Outlines and character coverage are unchanged. Internal names have been renamed
to ZYAN Ming Regular and ZYAN Ming SemiBold. Full-font WOFF2 conversion only.
Distributed under SIL OFL 1.1; see OFL.txt. No proprietary MingLiU is included.
The only named families are ZYAN Ming and system Courier New. Real Regular
(400) and SemiBold (600) faces are supplied for CJK; font synthesis is disabled.
