ZYAN Ming ExtraLight is derived from Adobe Source Han Serif SC ExtraLight.
Source commit: 7889f11bf31170b5d092a083b357c8c8130f89e0
https://github.com/adobe-fonts/source-han-serif
Outlines and full character coverage are unchanged. Internal names are renamed
to ZYAN Ming ExtraLight. Converted to WOFF2 under SIL OFL 1.1; see OFL.txt.
This is the same source and weight (250) used by the website webfont,
but with full coverage for arbitrary Typora documents instead of a web subset.
MingLiU is not used or distributed. Courier New is a system font.
